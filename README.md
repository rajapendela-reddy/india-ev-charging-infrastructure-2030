# India EV Charging Infrastructure — 2030 Investment Opportunity

## 📌 Project Overview

India's EV adoption is expected to expand significantly by 2030, creating a corresponding need for charging infrastructure.

This project evaluates the **state-wise investment opportunity in India's EV charging infrastructure market through 2030** by combining historical EV registrations, infrastructure availability, scenario-based EV projections, infrastructure gap analysis, and investor opportunity scoring.

The analysis identifies **where charging infrastructure investment should be prioritized** and estimates the potential infrastructure and capital requirements under Conservative, Base, and Aggressive EV adoption scenarios.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

> Which Indian states offer the strongest EV charging infrastructure investment opportunities through 2030, considering projected EV demand, infrastructure shortfall, and market concentration?

### Objectives

- Estimate India's EV market through 2030 under multiple scenarios.
- Estimate the charging infrastructure required to support future EV adoption.
- Identify states with the largest infrastructure gaps.
- Develop a state-level Investor Opportunity Score.
- Estimate potential infrastructure investment requirements.
- Present the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
